Chapter 8Inventory: medicine stock

Inventory keeps count of every medicine. Stock goes down by itself when a bill or refill is paid, so you only touch it when something arrives, moves, breaks or is counted.

The Stock tab

{{fig:inventory-stock-all|The stock list with its counters at the top.}}

At the top right are three totals: Medicines in the list, Low stock and Expiry alerts. Each medicine shows its unit price and how many are at the Counter (the dispensing desk) and in the Storeroom, plus the Total stock and Expiry.

  • Type in Search medicines… to find one.
  • Use the filters All medicines, Low stock and Expiry alerts to see only what needs attention.
  • A negative number means more was supplied than was recorded: a sign to do a count (see below).
{{pair:inventory-low-stock|The Low stock filter.;;inventory-stock|Stock for the counter and storeroom.}}

Recording a purchase

When a delivery arrives from a supplier press + Record purchase.

  1. Choose the Supplier and type the Invoice number; check the Date.
  2. Choose the Stock point where the boxes go (for example the Counter or the Storeroom).
  3. Under Drugs received pick each drug, type the Qty (quantity) and the Cost price (₹). Press + Add another drug if the delivery has several medicines.
  4. Save the purchase. The stock numbers rise straight away.
{{fig:inventory-record-purchase|Recording a purchase.|half}}

Moving stock from storeroom to counter

Press Transfer. Choose the Drug, say From and To where it moves, type the Quantity and an optional note, and press Transfer. The total does not change; only the place does.

{{fig:inventory-transfer|Transferring stock between stock points.|half}}

Adjusting stock (damage, loss, correction)

Press Adjust. Choose the Drug and Stock point, and type the Change: use a minus sign to remove stock (for example -2). Type a Reason (for example "damaged in transit") and press Adjust. The reason is saved in the history.

{{fig:inventory-adjust|Adjusting stock with a reason.|half}}

Monthly count

Once a month, count what is really on the shelf. Press Monthly count, choose the Stock point, you will see a table with Expected (what iClinic thinks you have), Counted (type what you actually count) and Gap (the difference). Fill in the counted numbers and press Confirm count. iClinic corrects the numbers and records the differences.

{{fig:inventory-monthly-count|Starting a monthly count.}}

Medicine units and prices

This tab lists each medicine with its unit, unit price, the Low stock at number that triggers a warning, and its expiry. A medicine marked unit not confirmed still needs checking: type how it is sold (tablet, strip, bottle…) and press Confirm unit. Press Edit price to change a price. Billing will ask the doctor to review quantities if the unit is not yet confirmed.

{{fig:inventory-units-prices|Units and prices.}}

Suppliers

The Suppliers tab is the list of companies you buy from. Add a supplier before you record a purchase from them.
