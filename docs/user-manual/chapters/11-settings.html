Chapter 11Settings Doctor / Admin

Settings is where the clinic is set up: prices, templates, logins and clinic details. Only doctors and administrators see it. Changes here affect everybody, so please change things carefully.

{{fig:settings-home|Settings. The list on the left switches between sections.}}

Queue

Three simple options that control how the queue behaves:

No-show reminder (minutes)How long a patient may stay Not here before reception is reminded. Called but not sent in (minutes)How long a called patient may wait before an alert appears. Closing time (IST)When the end-of-day checklist appears.
{{fig:settings-queue|Queue settings.}}

Screen lock

Below the queue options is a tick box, Lock the screen automatically when nobody is using it. It is ticked by default (reception locks after 2 minutes, doctors after 15). Untick it to turn the automatic lock off on every computer in the clinic: patient names then stay on screen until someone presses Lock. The Lock button keeps working either way.

Think before you turn it off

The lock hides patient names from people walking past the desk. If you turn it off, remind staff to press Lock when they step away.

Medicines & prices

The full medicine list (Drugs). Press + Add drug to add one; type its name, type, price and when to warn about low stock. Remove takes a medicine off the list.

{{fig:settings-medicines|The drug list.}}

Procedures

The list of procedures the doctor performs, each with a price. These are what appear in the prescription's Procedures & bill approval search (Chapter 4).

{{fig:settings-procedures|Procedures and their prices.}}

Templates

Medicine sets for quick adding (Chapter 4). You can see, rename or remove them here.

{{fig:settings-templates|Prescription templates.}}

Custom fields

Extra questions on the new-patient form, such as Pain Score 0-10. Press + Add field, give it a label, and it appears on the form from then on. Remove takes it off the form (what was already typed is kept).

{{fig:settings-custom-fields|Custom patient fields.}}

Staff & access

The list of logins and the role of each. Press + Add login (top right) to create a login for a new person: type their email, choose a role (receptionist, doctor or admin) and give them a first password. Remove takes a role away.

{{fig:settings-staff|Logins and roles. The Add login button sits at the top right.}}
Use the right role

Give reception staff the receptionist role. Only give doctor or admin to people who really need Reports, Settings and prescriptions.

Clinic information

Details that print on receipts and QR codes: UPI ID (VPA), Consultation fee (₹), Doctor's name, Doctor's registration number, Clinic address and Clinic phone. If the UPI ID is wrong, the payment QR code will send money to the wrong place, so check it with care.
