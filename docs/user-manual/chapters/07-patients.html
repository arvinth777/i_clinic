Chapter 7Patients: records and history

The Patients page is the clinic's filing cabinet. Search for anyone who has ever visited and see their visits, prescriptions, notes and documents in one place.

Finding a patient

  1. Open Patients.
  2. Type part of a name or phone number in the search box on the left.
  3. Click the person in Search results. Their record opens on the right.
{{fig:patients-directory|Search on the left, the selected patient's record on the right.}}

At the top of the record you see the name, age, sex, height, weight, phone and address, followed by three quick facts: number of Visits, Last visit and the Review date (the follow-up the doctor set).

Updating height and weight

Press Update height and weight under the patient's details, type the new numbers and save. The new values show on the next prescription. Height and weight are checked to be sensible, so a typing slip such as 1720 cm is caught.

{{fig:patients-update-body|Updating height and weight.}}

The four tabs

Visits & prescriptions

Every visit, newest first, with the diagnosis, the medicines and the follow-up date. View receipt → opens the bill for that visit. Doctors see the full prescription; reception sees the list of visits.

{{fig:patients-record-doctor-visits|The doctor's view of visits and prescriptions.}} {{fig:patients-record-reception|Reception's view of the same record.}}

Timeline

A single list of everything that has happened to this patient, in date order: registrations, visits, prescriptions, documents, notes, payments and so on. It is the quickest way to answer "what happened last time?".

{{fig:patients-timeline-doctor|The timeline shows the whole story in order.}}

Personal notes

Private notes for the doctor about this patient. Reception does not see them.

{{fig:patients-notes|Personal notes.}}

Documents

Two lists live here. Issued PDFs are copies of prescriptions, bills and certificates that iClinic made. Below them, you can upload scans, photos and reports (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 25 MB each) such as an MRI report:

  1. Choose the File.
  2. Type a Title (for example Lumbar spine MRI report), pick the Category, and optionally add the Examination date and a Note.
  3. Save. Later you can View, Download or Open in Drive.

Files are kept in the clinic's Google Drive. A clinic administrator connects Drive once with Connect Google Drive. If a file could not be saved yet, it shows Not saved to Drive yet; press Retry pending uploads when you are online again.
