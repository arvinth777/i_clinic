Chapter 3Reception: running the queue

Once patients are checked in, the doctor calls them one by one and reception sends them in. This chapter explains that hand-off and the small actions for when a patient steps out.

How a patient reaches the doctor

  1. The doctor presses Call #… on the Doctor page. On the reception screen that patient's row changes to Called · waiting for reception.
  2. Reception asks the patient to go in, and presses Sent in when they walk into the consultation room.
  3. The row now says With doctor. The doctor can press Start prescription.
{{fig:reception-called-send-in|Token #3067 has been called. Reception presses Sent in when the patient goes inside.}}
Why "Sent in"?

It is a safety step. The doctor can call a token, but the visit only starts once reception confirms the patient is really there. If a called patient is not sent in for several minutes, an alert reminds the desk (you can change that time in Settings → Queue).

If someone is not at the desk

Press Not here on a patient's row if they have stepped out (for tea, for a phone call, to move their car). The row moves to a Not here state and the doctor moves on to the next person. You can then choose:

ButtonWhat it does
Return to positionThe patient is back; they go to the place they had in the queue.
Return at endThe patient is back, but goes to the end of the line.
Mark no-showThe patient has gone home. The visit is closed.

After a while iClinic gently reminds you about someone who has been marked Not here for too long (the default is set in Settings → Queue, No-show reminder (minutes)).

Changing the order

For a waiting patient you can press Move up or Move down to change their place, for example for an elderly patient or an emergency. The box Move reason (optional) above the list lets you type a short reason; it is saved so that anyone can see later why the order changed (you will see "moved up by reception" under the patient's name).

Tip

The doctor still chooses who to call. Moving someone up changes where they sit in the waiting list the doctor sees.

Old visits left open

If a patient was checked in on an earlier day and the visit was never finished, it is not counted in today's queue. Open the tab Earlier open visits to see them. Open the visit and finish it (or mark it no-show) so that the numbers in the reports stay honest. The doctor's workspace shows the number as Left open from earlier days.

What reception does at the end of the day

  • Open Alerts and look at the Closing checklist.
  • Clear Ready to bill rows by billing them (Chapter 5).
  • Look at Earlier open visits and close what is finished.
  • Lock the screen, or sign out if the computer is shared (Chapter 1).
