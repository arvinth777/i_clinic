Chapter 10The More menu

Less common jobs live under More. What you see depends on your role.

Unpaid bills

Every bill that was left as Pay later / Credit is listed here with the patient, token, the date billed and the amount. When the money arrives:

  1. Open More → Unpaid bills and press Settle on the right row.
  2. Under Paid via choose Cash or UPI.
  3. Add a note if you like (for example "paid in person on next visit"), then press Mark settled.

Long-term care

A register of patients the doctor has marked for ongoing care (set in the prescription's Review step). It shows Name, Last visit and Next review due; a date in amber means the review is due or overdue. Use it to phone patients who need to come back.

Merge patients Doctor

Sometimes the same person gets registered twice. Merging combines the two records into one so that the history is complete.

{{fig:merge-home|Merge duplicate patients.}}
  1. Open More → Merge patients.
  2. Under Possible duplicates iClinic lists pairs with the same name and age or phone. Press Review to compare a pair.
  3. If they are the same person, confirm the merge. The older record is always kept and the newer one's visits and notes move into it.
  4. If they are different people, press Not a duplicate. That pair will not be suggested again.
{{fig:merge-review-pair|Reviewing a pair before merging.}}
Be careful

A merge cannot be undone with one click. Merging is blocked while either patient has an open visit today. A matching name or phone is only a hint: two relatives can share a phone number. Always read the pair before you confirm.

Needs reconciliation Doctor

If a bill was finalised while the internet was down and the price changed before it synced, it lands here. The page shows the patient, token, date, Billed as and the Actual amount. Press Correct to write a new bill using the supplied quantities and the doctor's current discount. The original receipt is not changed. Rows that say Review required cannot be corrected automatically and need the doctor's attention.
