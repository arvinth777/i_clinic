Chapter 1Getting started

iClinic runs the whole day of the clinic in one place: who has arrived, who the doctor sees next, what was prescribed, what was paid and what is left in the medicine cupboard. This chapter shows how to sign in and find your way around.

What iClinic does

Think of one patient visit as a short relay. Each person does their own part, and the next person sees it straight away on their screen.

ReceptionRegisters the patient and gives a token number
DoctorCalls the patient, writes the prescription
ReceptionSees the prescription arrive in Billing
BillingCollects payment, hands over medicines

You never have to carry paper from one desk to the next. When the doctor presses Finish consultation, the bill appears at the reception desk.

Signing in

  1. Open the clinic's iClinic web address in your browser (on the computer at your desk, or on a phone).
  2. Type your email and password, then press Sign in.
  3. You land on the first page you need: Reception for the front desk, Doctor for the doctor.

Who gives me a login?

The clinic owner or an administrator creates logins in Settings → Staff & access (see Chapter 11). If your password does not work, ask them to reset it. Please never share a login: every action is recorded under the name of the person who did it.

What you will see: your role decides

iClinic shows each person only the pages that belong to their job. A receptionist does not see the doctor's prescription page, and the doctor's reports are hidden from reception.

Using a phone

iClinic also works on a phone. The page names fold into a Menu button, and each page stacks into one column. Everything in this manual works the same way on a phone.

Locking the screen

Patient names are private. iClinic locks itself if nobody touches it for a while (2 minutes on a reception login, 15 minutes on a doctor login). You can also lock it yourself with Lock in the top bar.

To unlock, type your PIN. To set it up the first time, open your name menu and choose Set a lock PIN, then type a PIN of 4 to 6 digits.

Tip

Anything you were typing stays exactly as it was. Locking does not lose your work.

Alerts

Press Alerts to see what needs attention: for example a patient who was called but not sent in, or an end-of-day checklist. Each alert has Open (takes you there) and Dismiss (hides it). You can also turn on a notification sound for new alerts.
