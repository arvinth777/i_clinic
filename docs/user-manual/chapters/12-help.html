Chapter 12When things go wrong

If the internet drops

iClinic is built to keep working when the connection is poor. What you already loaded stays on the screen, and what you save is kept on this computer until the connection returns, then sent automatically. A banner at the top tells you what is waiting.

  • All saved in the top bar: everything is safely stored.
  • A banner saying something is not saved yet: your change is waiting in this browser. Keep the page open (or at least the same browser) until it says saved.
Please do not clear browser data

Clearing history, cookies or "site data" while changes are waiting would delete them. If the banner shows unsaved changes, wait for it to clear first.

A change was rejected (stuck changes)

Occasionally the server refuses a waiting change, for example because it was already handled on another computer. The banner then says the change is on hold and gives two choices.

ButtonWhen to use it
RetryTry again. Use this first, especially after the internet came back.
Discard this changeRemove that one change for good. A confirmation box asks you to be sure. Use it only if you know the work was already done another way.

Quick fixes

ProblemWhat to do
I cannot sign inCheck the email, and that Caps Lock is off. If it still fails, ask the administrator to reset the password.
The screen is locked and I forgot my PINSign out from the lock screen and sign in again with your password, then set a new PIN.
A page I need is missingYour role may not include it. Ask the administrator.
A patient has two tokens todayAsk the doctor to open More → Merge patients if there are two records, or finish the extra visit as no-show.
A medicine is not in the searchAsk the doctor or administrator to add it in Settings → Medicines & prices.
Stock shows a negative numberMore was supplied than was recorded. Do a Monthly count (Chapter 8).
The page looks out of dateIt refreshes by itself every half minute. You can also reload the browser; nothing saved is lost.
Wrong amount on a billAsk the doctor to change the final amount before payment. After payment, use the correction process (Chapter 10).

Appendix BQuick reference by role

Reception: a day at the desk

  1. Sign in. Open Reception.
  2. Patient arrives: search first, then Check in (or Register new patient).
  3. Doctor calls a token: press Sent in when the patient goes in.
  4. Patient steps out: Not here; when back, Return to position or Return at end.
  5. Doctor finishes: Bill → check supplied quantities → payment method → Mark paid → print.
  6. Medicine-only customer: Refills.
  7. End of day: Alerts → closing checklist; clear Earlier open visits; lock or sign out.

Doctor: a consultation

  1. Open Doctor and press Call #….
  2. When reception sends the patient in: Start prescription.
  3. Diagnosis, medicines (or a template), advice, follow-up date.
  4. Procedures and Approve bill.
  5. Review prescription → Confirm prescription → Finish consultation.
