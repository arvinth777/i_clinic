Chapter 4Doctor: seeing patients and writing prescriptions

The Doctor page is the doctor's workspace. It shows the patient in front of you on the left and the waiting list on the right. When you finish, the prescription and bill go straight to reception.

The workspace

{{fig:doctor-workspace|The doctor's workspace: the current patient (left), the waiting list (right).}}
  • Left side: the patient's name, age, sex, height, weight, phone and address; today's complaint from reception; the last prescription; past visits (Full record → opens the whole history); and Personal notes that only the doctor sees.
  • Right side: Waiting with the number waiting and how many were seen today, the black Call #… button for the next token, and each waiting patient's complaint.
  • The note Left open from earlier days counts visits from previous days that were never finished. They are not in today's queue; reception can review them.

Calling the next patient

  1. Press the black Call #… button. It calls the next token in the waiting list.
  2. The workspace shows Called · waiting for reception to send this patient in.
  3. When reception presses Sent in, the patient's details open and Start prescription → appears.
{{fig:doctor-called-waiting|After pressing Call, the doctor waits for reception to send the patient in.}}
Personal notes

Under the patient's history is a private notes box. Press Add note to write something for yourself, such as "prefers morning appointments". Notes are for the doctor only and also show on the patient's record (Chapter 7).

Writing the prescription

Press Start prescription →. The editor opens. On a large screen the A4 preview of the printed prescription is shown on the right and updates as you type; on a phone the editor takes the whole screen.

{{fig:doctor-prescription-empty|The prescription editor, ready to type.|half}}

Step 1: Diagnosis

Type the diagnosis in the Diagnosis box. The patient's complaint from reception is already shown above it.

Step 2: Add medicines

Start typing a medicine's name in the search box and click it in the list.

{{fig:doctor-medicine-search|Search the medicine list by name.|half}}

The medicine is added as a row. Fill in how it is to be taken:

{{fig:doctor-medicine-row|One medicine row.|half}}
TypeTablet, capsule, syrup, injection and so on. StrengthFor example 500 mg. FoodBefore food, after food, with food. FrequencyHow many times a day, such as 1-0-1, or SOS (only when needed). DurationHow many days. QuantityHow many the patient should be supplied with, so that Billing knows what to hand over. NoteAnything special for this medicine.
{{fig:doctor-prescription-filled|Diagnosis filled in and the first medicine added. The Review prescription button stays at the bottom.|half}}

Quick add with templates

If you often prescribe the same set of medicines, use a template. Pick a template under Quick add and all its medicines are added in one go. You can still change anything afterwards.

{{fig:doctor-template-added|A template adds its medicines in one click.|half}}

To make a new template, put the medicines you want on the prescription, press Save as template, give it a name, and save. Templates remember what to prescribe, not how many to supply.

{{fig:doctor-save-template|Saving the current medicines as a template.|half}}

Step 3: Advice

Type advice and investigations (exercises, rest, tests to do). It prints on the prescription.

Step 4: Review: follow-up and long-term care

In the Review part, choose the Follow-up date and press Save follow-up date. Reception will see the patient in Follow-ups due today on that day. If the patient needs regular care, tick Long-term patient and type the Review interval (days), which is how often they should come back (see Chapter 10).

{{fig:doctor-review-care|Follow-up date and long-term care settings.}}

Procedures and the bill amount

If the doctor did a procedure (for example an injection or nerve block), add it in Procedures & bill approval. Search for the procedure, add it, and iClinic calculates the Calculated total from the clinic's price list. You then set the amount the patient will be charged:

  • Final amount: the amount to collect. You may change it.
  • Discount: if the final amount is lower than the calculated total, the difference is shown as a discount.
  • Press Approve bill. It then says Bill approved.
{{fig:doctor-procedures-pricing|Procedures, calculated total and final amount.}}
Only the doctor sets the amount

Reception cannot change the amount at the billing desk. This keeps the money side simple and honest: the doctor decides, billing collects.

{{fig:doctor-bill-approved|The bill approved, ready for billing.}}

Certificates, sick leave and referrals

Open the Documents section to issue a Certificate, Sick leave letter or Referral. Choose the type, type the Purpose (what it is for) and press Issue & print. A PDF copy is also filed in the clinic's Google Drive.

{{fig:doctor-documents-panel|Issuing a certificate, sick leave or referral.}}

Review, confirm and finish

  1. Press Review prescription to read the whole prescription once more, exactly as it will print.
  2. Press Confirm prescription. After this the prescription is issued and shown read-only.
  3. Press Finish consultation.
  4. iClinic says Prescription sent to billing. You can open View prescription or Print prescription from here.

The prescription is shown in read-only form afterwards, with the printable A4 view. Confirmed prescriptions are not edited casually, so check carefully before you confirm.

{{fig:doctor-issued-view|An issued prescription is read-only. Use Print prescription to print it again.|half}} {{fig:a4-prescription|The printed A4 prescription on the clinic's letterhead.|narrow}}
Tip: nothing is lost if the page closes

While you type, iClinic keeps a draft on this computer. If the browser closes or the internet drops, come back to the same patient and your draft is still there.

{{fig:doctor-workspace-sent|Back in the workspace, ready to call the next patient.}}
