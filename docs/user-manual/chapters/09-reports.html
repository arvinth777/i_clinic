Chapter 9Reports

Reports answer "how did we do?". They are for the doctor and administrator and appear on the Reports page. They are read-only: nothing here changes any record.

Daily

{{fig:reports-daily|The Daily report.}}
BoxMeaning
Collections todayMoney received today.
Patients todayPatients seen today.
Discounts given todayThe total reduction the doctor allowed.
Bills needing reconciliationBills that need a check (see Chapter 10).
Corrections todayNumber of corrections made, and their net effect.

Below the numbers, Stock warnings lists medicines that are low or have gone below zero.

Monthly

A bar chart of collections for the last six months and a table of Collections, Patients and Discounts (subsidised care) for each month. It is a quick view of how the clinic is trending.

{{fig:reports-monthly|Monthly collections.}}

GST / tax

Choose a From and To date, press Refresh and see Collections, Discounts and the number of Bills for the period. Press Export CSV to download a spreadsheet file that you can send to the accountant.
