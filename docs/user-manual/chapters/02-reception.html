Chapter 2Reception: registering and checking in

The Reception page is the front desk's home. The big list on the left is Today's queue. The panel on the right is where you check people in.

The Reception page

Every patient who comes in today gets a token number (for example #3067). The queue shows the tokens in the order the patients will be seen, with the patient's name, age, a short complaint, how long they have waited, and what stage they are at.

{{fig:reception-home|Today's queue (left) and the Check in panel (right).}}

The tabs above the list let you choose what to look at: Active (everyone still to be seen or billed), Done today, All today, and Earlier open visits (visits from earlier days that were never closed). Sort reorders the list by token, name, stage or waiting time.

A new patient: register and check in

Use this the very first time a person visits the clinic.

  1. In the right panel press Register new patient.
  2. Fill in the form (the table below says which fields are needed).
  3. Press Check in. The patient gets the next token number and appears at the bottom of the queue.
FieldNotes
Name, Age, GenderNeeded. Type the name the way the patient says it, so it is easy to search for later.
Height (cm), Weight (kg)Optional, but useful: they print on the prescription. iClinic checks the numbers are sensible (for example it will not accept a height of 17 cm).
Address / Village, PhonePhone is how you find the patient next time, so please enter it carefully.
ComplaintOne short line in the patient's own words. The doctor sees it first.
Pain Score 0–10A clinic-specific field. The administrator can add or change fields like this (Chapter 11).

A returning patient: search and check in

  1. Click the search box in the Check in panel and type part of the name, the phone number or the token.
  2. Click the right person in the results.
  3. Type today's Complaint and press Check in.
{{fig:reception-search-results|Search finds patients by name, phone or token.|narrow}}
"Already in today's queue"

If the person is already in today's queue, iClinic tells you so, for example Already in today's queue as #3063 (With doctor). Check in only if this is a new visit. Press Cancel unless the patient really is coming for a second, separate visit today. This stops people being given two tokens by accident.

{{fig:reception-existing-checkin|The warning for a patient who is already checked in.|narrow}}
Tip: don't create the patient twice

Always search first. If the same person ends up registered twice, the doctor can join the two records later (see Merge patients in Chapter 10), but it is much easier to avoid it.

Pharma representatives

Medical representatives who come to see the doctor can be put in the queue too, so that they do not walk in ahead of patients. Press Check in pharma rep, type the Rep name and Company, and press Check in. They get a token like anyone else and are called in turn.

{{fig:reception-pharma-rep|Checking in a pharma representative.|narrow}}

Follow-ups due today

When a doctor sets a follow-up date, the patient shows up here on that day. Press Follow-ups due today to see the list of people who should come back. Press Done next to a name once you have dealt with it (called them, or they arrived).
